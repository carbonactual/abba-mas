# Relationship Fabric Conformance V1

ABBA MAS supplies capabilities to ABBA. It may observe, score, correlate and recommend relationship-aware actions, but it does not issue authority and does not redefine the relationship graph.

## Required boundaries

- Capability does not become authority.
- Intelligence does not become authority.
- A relationship does not become consent.
- A Pulse/evidence signal does not become authority.
- Relationship matching does not authorize execution.

## Runtime rule

ABBA MAS may consume canonical relationship references and produce capability/observation signals. Consequential execution returns through ABBA/OMNII and the existing Authority/Consent/Verification gates.

## No duplication

ABBA MAS must not create a competing authoritative human or organization relationship store. Derived relationship features must preserve source relationship identifiers and provenance.
