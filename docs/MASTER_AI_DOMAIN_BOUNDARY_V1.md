# ABBA MAS Master AI Domain Boundary v1

ABBA MAS is the governed coordination and execution fabric of ABBA Master AI.

It owns command intake, capability classification, routing, SEAL evaluation, worker coordination, provider/tool policy, execution leases, telemetry, verification and Proof/Actual handoff.

It does not become a second constitution or second master AI.

OMNII supplies universal constitutional/runtime contracts. ABBA Master AI supplies intelligence and orchestration. HAPI supplies identity/authority context where applicable. I/O supplies movement and settlement routes. Pulse, Proof, Actual, Ash and Phoenix provide feedback, evidence, formed state and recovery lifecycle.

ABBA-related forks are capability sources. Useful skills, agents, workflows, prompts, provider adapters and tests may be registered and routed after license, security, provenance and capability validation. Forks do not silently become canonical.

New core execution capabilities must have a free/open-source, self-hostable or open-standard path. Commercial services are optional adapters and cannot be mandatory dependencies.

Activation lifecycle: candidate → inspected → compatible → registered → verified → enabled → monitored → paused/blocked → replaced/deprecated.
