# ABBA MAS Master AI Domain Boundary v1

ABBA MAS is the governed coordination/execution fabric of ABBA Master AI.

It owns command intake, capability classification, routing, SEAL evaluation, worker coordination, provider/tool policy, execution leases, telemetry, verification and Proof/Actual handoff.

It does not become a second constitution or second master AI.

## Parent contracts

OMNII supplies universal constitutional/runtime contracts. ABBA Master AI supplies intelligence and orchestration. HAPI supplies identity/authority context where applicable. I/O supplies movement and settlement routes. Pulse, Proof, Actual, Ash and Phoenix provide feedback, evidence, formed state and recovery lifecycle.

## Fork integration

ABBA-related forks are capability sources. Their useful skills, agents, workflows, prompts, provider adapters and tests may be registered and routed through ABBA MAS after license, security, provenance and capability validation. Forks do not silently become canonical.

## Free/open constraint

Core execution must retain a free/open/self-hostable or open-standard route. Commercial services are optional adapters and cannot be mandatory dependencies.

## Activation lifecycle

`candidate → inspected → compatible → registered → verified → enabled → monitored → paused/blocked → replaced/deprecated`

Every enabled route requires current verification evidence and remains subject to SEAL, risk and rollback controls.
