# Ecosystem Conformance

ABBA MAS is the command, routing, SEAL and proof coordination fabric for ABBA Master AI.

It owns structured command intake, classification, policy/SEAL routing, worker/provider selection, result verification and proof envelopes.

It does not become a second ABBA intelligence, a constitutional source, or a provider itself. External services are adapters; capabilities are registered separately from providers; every consequential action is attributable and policy-gated.
